# Dragon Ball FAQ

A short set of common questions about the Dragon Ball franchise, with brief
general-knowledge answers.

## Who created Dragon Ball?

Dragon Ball was created by Akira Toriyama. The manga was serialized in
Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump from 1984 to 1995.

## What is the difference between Dragon Ball and Dragon Ball Z?

Dragon Ball follows Son Goku's childhood and early martial-arts adventures.
Dragon Ball Z picks up with Goku as an adult and shifts toward larger battles
involving his Saiyan heritage.

## What are the Dragon Balls?

The Dragon Balls are seven mystical orbs that, when gathered together, summon a
dragon that can grant wishes. They are the central plot device the franchise is
named after.

## Who is Goku?

Son Goku is the main protagonist of the series. He is a Saiyan raised on Earth
who trains as a martial artist and defends the planet against powerful foes.

## What is a Super Saiyan?

Super Saiyan is a transformation that greatly increases a Saiyan's power, marked
by golden hair and a glowing aura. It first appears during the events of
Dragon Ball Z.

## Is Dragon Ball GT based on the manga?

No. Dragon Ball GT is an anime-original sequel produced after the original manga
ended, rather than a direct adaptation of Toriyama's manga.
