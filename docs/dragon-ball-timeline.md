# Dragon Ball Franchise Timeline

A brief, non-exhaustive chronology of major franchise milestones, written from
general knowledge. Dates are approximate, have not been verified here, and no
sources are cited.

1. **1984** — The *Dragon Ball* manga begins serialization in *Weekly Shōnen Jump* (running to 1995).
2. **1986** — The *Dragon Ball* anime airs, adapting the earlier manga story (to 1989).
3. **1989** — *Dragon Ball Z* begins broadcast, continuing the story into the "Z" era (to 1996).
4. **1996** — *Dragon Ball GT* airs as an anime-original sequel (to 1997).
5. **2013** — The film *Dragon Ball Z: Battle of Gods* is released.
6. **2015** — The film *Dragon Ball Z: Resurrection 'F'* is released.
7. **2015** — The *Dragon Ball Super* anime begins airing (to 2018).
8. **2018** — The film *Dragon Ball Super: Broly* is released.
9. **2022** — The film *Dragon Ball Super: Super Hero* is released.
10. **2024** — The anime series *Dragon Ball Daima* airs (into 2025).
