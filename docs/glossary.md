# Dragon Ball Glossary

A brief list of common Dragon Ball terms.

## Dragon Balls

Seven mystical orbs that summon a wish-granting dragon when gathered together.

## Ki

The life energy that fighters sense, channel, and project as powerful attacks.

## Namekian

A green-skinned alien species from Planet Namek, known for regeneration and fusion.

## Saiyan

A warrior race from Planet Vegeta with great strength and a natural urge to fight.

## Senzu Bean

A magical bean that fully restores a person's strength and heals their injuries.

## Super Saiyan

A legendary Saiyan transformation marked by golden hair and greatly increased power.

## Z-Fighter

A member of the informal group of warriors who defend Earth alongside Goku.

## Zenkai

A power boost a Saiyan gains after recovering from near-fatal injuries.
