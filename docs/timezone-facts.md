# Timezone Facts

A short illustrative sample of nine timezones. All offsets below are
**standard-time offsets**: fixed values that ignore daylight saving time, so no
entry is shifted for summer time. The list is a small sample rather than a
reference dataset, and the offsets should not be treated as authoritative or
complete.

## New York

- **UTC offset:** UTC-05:00.
- **Observation:** The eastern seaboard's business day starts five hours behind UTC.

## London

- **UTC offset:** UTC+00:00.
- **Observation:** The prime meridian runs through the city, so standard time matches UTC.

## Berlin

- **UTC offset:** UTC+01:00.
- **Observation:** Central European time sits one hour ahead of UTC across the continent.

## Kolkata

- **UTC offset:** UTC+05:30.
- **Observation:** The half-hour offset reflects a compromise between India's eastern and western regions.

## Nairobi

- **UTC offset:** UTC+03:00.
- **Observation:** East Africa keeps a single offset year-round despite spanning a wide longitude range.

## Tokyo

- **UTC offset:** UTC+09:00.
- **Observation:** Japan's standard time is nine hours ahead and is kept without seasonal changes.

## Sydney

- **UTC offset:** UTC+10:00.
- **Observation:** The eastern Australian standard offset is ten hours ahead of UTC.

## Honolulu

- **UTC offset:** UTC-10:00.
- **Observation:** Hawaii's standard offset is one of the furthest behind UTC in common use.

## Reykjavik

- **UTC offset:** UTC+00:00.
- **Observation:** Iceland uses UTC as its standard offset throughout the year.
