# Common Garden Birds

A short illustrative list of ten birds often seen in gardens. Sizes are rough
approximate ranges and the timing given is a general guide rather than a
survey result. This is not a field guide, it is not scoped to any particular
region, and it makes no claim to be complete or authoritative.

## European Robin

- **Name:** European Robin.
- **Approximate size:** about 12.5 to 14 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, and often tame around garden borders.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the orange-red breast and face.

## Blue Tit

- **Name:** Blue Tit.
- **Approximate size:** about 10.5 to 12 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, and a frequent visitor to feeders in winter.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the blue cap and wings with yellow underparts.

## House Sparrow

- **Name:** House Sparrow.
- **Approximate size:** about 14 to 16 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, often in noisy small flocks.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the male's grey crown and chestnut nape.

## Common Blackbird

- **Name:** Common Blackbird.
- **Approximate size:** about 23 to 29 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, with song most noticeable in spring.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the male's all-black plumage and orange-yellow bill.

## Common Starling

- **Name:** Common Starling.
- **Approximate size:** about 19 to 22 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, gathering into larger flocks in winter.
- **Distinguishing feature:** glossy, iridescent plumage with pale speckles.

## European Goldfinch

- **Name:** European Goldfinch.
- **Approximate size:** about 12 to 13 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, often feeding on seed heads in autumn and winter.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the red face and bright yellow wing bar.

## Common Wood Pigeon

- **Name:** Common Wood Pigeon.
- **Approximate size:** about 40 to 45 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, and often on lawns or under feeders.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the white neck patch and pinkish-grey breast.

## Common Chaffinch

- **Name:** Common Chaffinch.
- **Approximate size:** about 14 to 16 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, with peak song in spring and early summer.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the male's reddish-brown breast and white wing bars.

## Great Tit

- **Name:** Great Tit.
- **Approximate size:** about 13.5 to 15 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, and a regular feeder visitor in winter.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the black head with white cheeks and yellow belly.

## Dunnock

- **Name:** Dunnock.
- **Approximate size:** about 13 to 14.5 cm long.
- **When typically seen:** year-round, most conspicuous in spring when singing.
- **Distinguishing feature:** the grey-brown streaked back and thin dark bill.
