# Dragon Ball Characters

A quick-reference table of major characters from the Dragon Ball franchise.

## Main characters

| Name | Role | First appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Son Goku | Protagonist and martial artist | Dragon Ball chapter 1 (1984) |
| Bulma | Inventor and early traveling companion | Dragon Ball chapter 1 (1984) |
| Krillin | Martial artist and close ally | Dragon Ball chapter 25 (1985) |
| Master Roshi | Martial arts teacher and mentor | Dragon Ball chapter 3 (1984) |
| Piccolo | Rival turned ally and mentor | Dragon Ball chapter 161 (1988) |
| Vegeta | Saiyan prince and rival turned ally | Dragon Ball chapter 204 (1988) |
| Frieza | Major antagonist of the Namek arc | Dragon Ball chapter 247 (1990) |
| Gohan | Goku's son and scholar-warrior | Dragon Ball chapter 196 (1988) |
| Cell | Bio-engineered antagonist of the Android arc | Dragon Ball chapter 361 (1992) |
| Majin Buu | Ancient magical antagonist of the Buu arc | Dragon Ball chapter 460 (1994) |
