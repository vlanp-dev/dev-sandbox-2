# Meeting Agenda Template

A reusable agenda for a focused meeting, with fixed sections and indicative
time allocations that you can adjust to fit the session.

## How to use this template

Fill in the meeting details and target length before you start. Treat every
duration below as guidance rather than a fixed rule: keep, reorder, shorten,
or delete sections so they match the purpose of the meeting, then note the
revised total. Replace each placeholder prompt with the specific items for
this meeting, and make sure every action item has a named owner and a due
date.

- Meeting title: [title]
- Date and time: [date and time]
- Owner: [name]
- Target length: [total minutes]

The sections below add up to an indicative 60 minutes.

## Welcome and context (5 min)

- Purpose of this meeting: [one sentence].
- Outcome we want by the end: [outcome].
- Context participants need before we start: [notes].

## Review of previous actions (5 min)

- Carried-over action: [action]. Owner: [name]. Status: [status].
- Blocked or overdue item: [item] and what it needs to move.
- Anything to close or drop: [item].

## Discussion item one (15 min)

- Topic: [topic].
- Why it matters now: [reason].
- Options to consider: [option one], [option two].
- Open question for the group: [question].

## Discussion item two (15 min)

- Topic: [topic].
- Why it matters now: [reason].
- Options to consider: [option one], [option two].
- Open question for the group: [question].

## Decisions (10 min)

- Decision: [what was decided].
- Rationale: [why].
- Owner of the follow-through: [name].

## Action items (5 min)

- Action: [action]. Owner: [name]. Due: [date].
- Action: [action]. Owner: [name]. Due: [date].
- Action: [action]. Owner: [name]. Due: [date].

## Close (5 min)

- Recap of decisions and actions: [short summary].
- Parking lot for later: [item].
- Next check-in: [date or trigger].
