# Emoji Cheat Sheet

A small, usage-grouped reference for twenty common emoji. Each entry gives the
glyph, a short name, and a one-line note on when to reach for it.

## Status

- ✅ **Check mark** — Confirms that a task, check, or requirement is complete.
- ⏳ **Hourglass** — Signals that something is still in progress or waiting on a dependency.
- 🚫 **Prohibited** — Marks an action or path as blocked or not allowed.
- ⚠️ **Warning** — Flags a caveat, risk, or detail that needs extra care.
- 🎉 **Party popper** — Celebrates a milestone, release, or small win.

## Weather and nature

- ☀️ **Sun** — Shorthand for bright, clear conditions or a sunny outlook.
- 🌧️ **Cloud with rain** — Indicates rain, showers, or a wet forecast.
- ❄️ **Snowflake** — Points to cold weather, snow, or freezing conditions.
- 🌈 **Rainbow** — Suggests a hopeful turn after a rough patch.
- 🌙 **Crescent moon** — Marks nighttime, late hours, or the end of a day.

## Objects

- 📌 **Pushpin** — Pins an important note or reminder in place.
- 🔑 **Key** — Highlights the essential point or the thing that unlocks progress.
- 📎 **Paperclip** — Indicates an attachment or something supplementary.
- 💡 **Light bulb** — Introduces an idea, tip, or helpful suggestion.
- 🧰 **Toolbox** — Refers to tools, resources, or a kit for getting work done.

## Communication

- 💬 **Speech balloon** — Opens or invites a conversation or comment thread.
- 📣 **Megaphone** — Marks a broad announcement meant for a wide audience.
- ✉️ **Envelope** — Points to a direct message, email, or private note.
- 🤝 **Handshake** — Signals agreement, partnership, or a deal closed.
- 🙋 **Person raising hand** — Shows volunteering, asking a question, or requesting attention.
